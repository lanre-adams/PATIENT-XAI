# Model card — PATIENT-XAI v0.1.0 (`patientxai-0.1.0-mvp`)

> **Research demonstrator. Not a medical device. Not clinically validated. Synthetic data only.**

## Model details
| | Baseline | Longitudinal model |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Discrete-time survival logistic regression (person-period format, L2, C = 0.5) | GRU encoder (1 layer, 32 units) + MLP discrete-time hazard head |
| Inputs | Last observed (LOCF) vitals/labs, medication flags, static variables, SBP/HbA1c/BMI slopes, visit count, last imaging embedding | Full visit sequence: 9 standardised measurements, 2 medication flags, 3 missingness masks, 8-d imaging embedding, Δt since previous visit, time since first visit; static: sex, smoking |
| Output | Annual hazard for years 1–5 → cumulative risk | Same |
| Uncertainty | 30 bootstrap refits (5th–95th percentile) | Deep ensemble of 7 independently initialised members (5th–95th percentile) |
| Attribution | Exact w·x on the hazard log-odds scale | Integrated gradients (32 steps, all-zero standardised baseline), ensemble mean, with completeness check |
| Ablation | — | GRU ensemble without imaging inputs (3 members) |
| Training | scikit-learn `lbfgs` | Adam (lr 3e-3, wd 1e-4), batch 128, early stopping on validation NLL (patience 8, max 60 epochs), gradient clipping 1.0 |
| Seed | 20261007 | 20261007 + 101·m |

## Intended use
- Demonstrating a reproducible pipeline for personalised longitudinal risk, uncertainty, attribution and
  counterfactual simulation, as preparation for doctoral research.
- Teaching and discussing the gap between predictive "what-if" simulation and causal effects.

## Out-of-scope use
Any clinical, screening, triage, insurance, employment or individual decision; use with real patient data;
any claim about real-world disease risk.

## Training and evaluation data
Synthetic cohort only (see `DATA_CARD.md`). Temporal split by enrolment year: train 2015–2019,
validation 2020 (threshold selection, early stopping), test 2021–2022.

## Metrics
Generated by the pipeline and written to `artifacts/performance.json` and `docs/results.md`: AUROC, AUPRC,
Brier score (each with patient-bootstrap 95% CIs), ECE, calibration slope/intercept, sensitivity, specificity,
PPV, NPV and confusion matrix at a validation-chosen threshold, per horizon and by sex.
**No number in this card is typed by hand; read `docs/results.md`.**

## Key findings to carry forward (see docs/results.md for values)
1. The GRU ensemble does not beat the transparent baseline on held-out discrimination; intervals overlap.
2. Imaging inputs give no measurable gain even though they carry information by construction.
3. Model-based counterfactuals assign risk reductions to BMI and HbA1c changes that have **zero** causal
   effect in the simulator, and give the wrong sign for treatment initiation (confounding by indication).

## Ethical considerations and caveats
- Attributions describe the model, not mechanisms. IG depends on the chosen baseline.
- Ensemble spread is not a calibrated individual confidence interval and ignores distribution shift.
- Complete-case evaluation at each horizon (no IPCW). One temporal split; no external validation.
- A single sex split is a minimal subgroup check, not a fairness audit.
